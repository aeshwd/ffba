ACADEMY OPERATION POLICIES

To preserve our elite competitive structure and elite training metrics, all registered athletes must strictly adhere to our governance framework:

  • Attendance Protocol: Elite tier athletes must maintain a minimum 85% attendance index per calendar month. Absences must be verified via medical telemetry 24 hours prior to the session.

LIABILITY & COURT DISCLAIMERS

High-performance badminton training naturally involves intense cardiovascular loading and biomechanical strains. Registration requires recognition of the following operational realities:

  • Equipment Authentication: Players assume full responsibility for maintaining their rackets and structural protective gear. The academy is not responsible for equipment damage resulting from structural collisions on court.

ANTI-DOPING COMPLIANCE MATRIX

FNF Badminton Academy enforces a zero-tolerance policy regarding performance-enhancing drugs, maintaining full alignment with standard National and International Sports Anti-Doping Agencies:

  • Substance Bans: The use of any banned anabolic matrices, artificial oxygen carriers, or prohibited stimulants is strictly forbidden.
